One candidate of kmer_denovo_filter's discovery mode at a time: its own region and each junction partner's window side by side, the child over the father and the mother, the reads that cross the junction drawn across the panels. The candidate region is shaded blue, a partner region orange; the dashed line marks where the child's clipped reads agree the breakpoint is.

samplot view (after Belyeu et al.): coverage split by MAPQ (dark ≥ 20, light below), then every read pair as a line at its insert size and every split read as a dotted line at its span, coloured by the event the strands imply. Reads between chromosomes sit at the top. Pileup view: the alignments as bars, soft clips as orange stubs, mates joined by a grey line, cross-window links as arcs.

The k-mer evidence: reads carrying proband-unique k-mers (the run's informative BAM, dk:i:1) carry an orange halo, and their coverage is the orange layer. A junction the parents' reads also show is inherited; one only the child's informative reads show is de novo or somatic.

Verdicts are kept in this browser and, when the page is served by svreview serve, appended to verdicts.tsv beside the manifest as they are given; export downloads them as the same TSV (svreview verdicts merges it into the candidate table).

svreview is not samplot: the picture follows samplot (Belyeu JR, Nicholas TJ, Pedersen BS, et al. SV-plaudit: a cloud-based framework for manually curating thousands of structural variants. GigaScience 2018;7(7):giy064; github.com/ryanlayer/samplot), re-drawn in the browser for the trio and the k-mer evidence. For publication figures use samplot.
